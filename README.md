# 🎁 Happy Birthday, Maxi!

A little interactive birthday surprise for Maxi.

Live at: https://badassprasad.github.io/happy-birthday-baby/

## What it is

1. A welcome screen
2. 6 gift boxes to tap open, each revealing a sweet note
3. A cake with 5 candles to blow out and make a wish
4. A final celebration screen with confetti, balloons, a birthday message, and a photo gallery

## Photos

Photos live in the `photos/` folder and are shown in a gallery on the final celebration screen.
To add more, drop images into `photos/` and reference them in the `.photo-gallery` section of `index.html`.
